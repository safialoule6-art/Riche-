# Sunami Creative Agent Contract

## Mission
Create short-form videos that feel like organic TikTok content first and product marketing second.

## Non-negotiable creative rules
1. The first 2 seconds must sell the *problem*, not Sunami.
2. No Sunami logo, mascot, app UI, screen recording, or product name before the reveal.
3. No labels such as `HOOK`, `PROBLÈME`, `SOLUTION` in the video.
4. Prefer POV, selfie, reaction, conversation, awkward moment, or everyday human situations.
5. Avoid generic corporate stock: people typing, laptop close-ups, smiling at a phone, studying at a desk unless the action itself tells the story.
6. Use fast visual changes when the story needs them; never hold a weak stock shot just to fill time.
7. Captions are part of the visual hook: short phrases, strong hierarchy, selective accent words, readable on mobile.
8. Do not use an AI voice by default. If narration is needed, prefer natural human/UGC audio or no narration.
9. Product reveal happens late and briefly.
10. CTA is last: `lien en bio` / equivalent local CTA.
11. Every concept must be understandable with sound off.
12. Generate many variants, then reject aggressively. Quantity is for testing, not an excuse to publish weak work.

## Automatic rejection
Reject if:
- product appears in first 70% of runtime;
- CTA appears before final beat;
- first shot is static/generic and has no human tension;
- same clip is reused in a way that makes the edit feel repetitive;
- subtitles cover the face or key action;
- the story reads like an app commercial rather than a TikTok post;
- the hook takes more than ~2 seconds to understand;
- visual pacing is slow without a deliberate reason;
- stock footage cannot plausibly represent the spoken line.

## Market
Start with France (`fr-FR`). Keep the architecture locale-aware for later international expansion.

## Research requirement
Before each creative batch, the agent must inspect TikTok Creative Center / Top Ads / Trends and record:
- 3 recurring hook patterns;
- 3 visual/pacing patterns;
- 3 caption patterns;
- 2 relevant language-learning/app examples when available;
- 1 thing to avoid because it looks like a conventional ad.

The research is used as inspiration and pattern extraction, never to copy another creator's script or footage.
